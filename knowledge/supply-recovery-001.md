# Supply recovery and automatic science transport

This checkpoint continuation runs at normal speed without new gameplay pause
transitions. It is not a rocket completion or a competitive baseline. Exact
layouts, observations, jobs, source hashes and checkpoints remain private.

## Verified result

The wider inspection corrected an incomplete attack recovery. Additional missing
coal conveyors, the smelting coal drill and its power connection were rebuilt
with normal inventory and crafting. The final observation showed all eight iron
furnaces and four steam engines working. This is an operating snapshot, not a
sustained full-load throughput measurement.

The first spare-coal chest intercepted the boiler supply upstream. Its intake was
normally removed, and a new collection route takes surplus downstream of the
boiler consumers. The new chest accumulated coal while the engines worked.

A conveyor now collects science from the existing assemblers and supplies four
labs. Green packs reached every lab through native inserters. Red ingredient
supply remained stalled, however, and Engine research stopped at 23 percent.
Transport construction therefore does not establish autonomous science production.

A neutral wreck blocked the first science route. Native placement preflight
stopped that scheduler before the affected batch was submitted. A locally checked
detour completed; the failed phase remains in the record. The recovery scheduler
then verified its construction target and retained the factory defense watch.
The engineer finished at full health; no new real attack response was measured.

## Improvements to carry forward

- Verify complete supply paths after an attack, including missing drills, poles,
  belts, fuel and downstream output. The original thirteen-tile repair was partial.
- Prevent one ingredient from filling shared input chests. The circuit input
  chest filled with iron while cable supply was ineffective. Prefer direct feeds
  into recipe-limited machine inputs where the geometry permits them.
- Keep science ingredients replenished in useful batches and verify simultaneous
  red/green consumption. A conveyor carrying one colour is insufficient.
- Group construction and repairs by location to avoid repeated cross-factory trips.
- Keep the independent local reflex active when a production planner stops on
  preflight; add native factory damage/destruction events and measured recovery.
